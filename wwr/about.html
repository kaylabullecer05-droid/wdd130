Extreme Rapids Co.

At Extreme Rapids Co., Our purpose is to deliver exhilarating, safe rafting adventures, with safety as our top priority. Our mission is to share the thrill of the river while respecting nature. Our creed: Courage, Teamwork, Respect. Our motto: Paddle Hard, Live Extreme.

History

Extreme Rapids Co. was founded in 1998 by a group of river guides who loved adventure. Starting with one raft and 3 guides, we have grown into a premier white water rafting company, guiding thousands of thrill-seekers through the most challenging and beautiful rapids in the country.

Adventure Awaits You!

Join us for an unforgettable experience on the river! Whether you are a beginner or an expert, we have the perfect trip for you. Our experienced guides will ensure you have a safe and exciting adventure.
