Extreme Rapids Co.

At Extreme Rapids Co., our purpose is to deliver unforgettable white water rafting adventures! We invite you to join us and experience the thrill of the river.

History

Extreme Rapids Co. was founded in 1990 by a group of river enthusiasts who wanted to share their love for white water rafting. What started as a small guide service has grown into a premier rafting company.

For over 30 years, we have guided thousands of adventurers through exhilarating rapids, always prioritizing safety, fun, and respect for nature. Our experienced guides make every trip memorable.
